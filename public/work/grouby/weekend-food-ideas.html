Weekend food ideas you should try out

Food ideas are suggestions, inspirations, or concepts for meals, snacks, or recipes that can be made with available ingredients or for specific purposes, from figuring out what to cook to looking for inspiration, food ideas help spark creativity in the kitchen which will save you stress in the kitchen .with grouby, you can get the best food ideas that will save you stress in the kitchen within your budget and are very easy to prepare. Here are some weekend food ideas you should try out ranging from breakfast to dinner and snacks:

Akara and pap: Akara is a savory fried bean cake that is often paired with pap and custard for a hearty and satisfying meal.

Yam Porridge (Asaro): a comforting and filling dish for a weekend breakfast made with yam, tomatoes, onions, peppers, and palm oil.

Beans and Plantain ( Ewa Agoyin and Dodo): This is a simple yet hearty breakfast or lunch option prepared with a rich spicy sauce (Ewa Agoyin) and fried plantains (Dodo).

Moi Moi: It is mostly served with rice or as an aside dish to accompany a main meal made from blended beans, onions, peppers, and spices.
