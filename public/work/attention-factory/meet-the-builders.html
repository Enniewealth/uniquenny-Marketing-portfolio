Meet the Builders strategy

A community participation programme, from member introductions to collaborative AI projects.

Attention Factory Community Engagement Initiatives

Initiative 1: MEET THE BUILDERS

Objective

Introduce new and existing members to one another, encourage conversations, and move the community from passive readers to active participants.

Meet the Builders is also designed to help us identify members who are genuinely interested in learning, contributing, and collaborating. These members will become eligible for the next phase of the initiative, the Builders Spotlight.

Why we're doing this

Most members join the community, read posts, and rarely engage. Meet the Builders gives members a chance to introduce themselves, discover people with similar interests, and begin building relationships within the community.

It also keeps the community active outside of our regular teaching sessions and gives members a reason to stay engaged beyond attending Weekends of AI.

How it works

Once a week, members will be invited to introduce themselves using a simple format:

• Name

• Location

• What they do

• Why they're learning AI

• One thing they'd like to build with AI

Members will also be encouraged to reply to at least one other person's introduction. This helps start conversations, build relationships, and encourage member-to-member interaction instead of admin-to-member communication.

Expected outcomes

• More member-to-member conversations.

• A stronger sense of belonging.

• Better understanding of members' interests and skill levels.

• Easier identification of active members for future community initiatives.

• Increased participation within the community.

Success metrics

• Number of introductions.

• Number of replies between members.

• Number of active participants.

• Members who continue engaging after the introduction activity.

• Number of members who register for Weekends of AI after participating.

Initiative 2: MEET THE BUILDERS SPOTLIGHT

Purpose

The Builders Spotlight is designed to encourage members to move beyond learning by collaborating on real AI projects.

The initiative creates opportunities for members to build together, showcase their work, teach others, and gain recognition for their contributions.

How it works

After each Meet the Builders session, we'll identify members who have shown genuine interest and engagement.

From these members, we'll select participants to form two teams within each community.

Each team will receive the same AI project brief and a deadline to complete it.

Team members will collaborate, communicate, solve problems together, and present their final solution.

Selection criteria

To be eligible for the Builders Spotlight, members must:

• Be a member of the community.

• Register for the upcoming Weekends of AI session.

• Participate in Meet the Builders.

• Be active in the community.

• Be willing to collaborate with a team.

• Judging criteria

Projects will be assessed based on:

• Creativity

• Team collaboration

• Practicality

• Project completion

• Presentation

• Recognition

The teams with the most outstanding projects will receive the following:

• A feature on Attention Factory's social media platforms.

• Recognition within the community.

• An opportunity to teach the community how they built their project, including the tools they used, their process, lessons learned, and challenges they overcame.

The goal is to celebrate builders while encouraging other members to take action.

Expected impact

This initiative is expected to:

• Increase community engagement.

• Increase registrations for Weekends of AI.

• Encourage collaboration among members.

• Build a pipeline of future community speakers.

• Showcase projects created by community members.

• Improve member retention.

• Position Attention Factory as a practical, community-driven AI learning ecosystem.

• Implementation schedule

The initiative will run weekly across the three communities, respectively:

• Wednesday: Community 1

• Thursday: Community 2

• Friday: Community 3

This schedule gives each community dedicated time for introductions, team formation, and engagement while keeping the workload manageable.

The Builders Spotlight is intended to become a recurring community programme where every active member has the opportunity to participate over time. As more members complete projects and share their experiences, we'll build a growing network of community builders who not only learn AI but also teach, mentor, and inspire others.

This turns the initiative from a one-time activity into a long-term community system, which is far more valuable from a strategic perspective. I would confidently present this version to management because it clearly explains the problem, solution, execution plan, and business impact.
